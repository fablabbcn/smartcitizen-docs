# Updating the token

This guide will help you update your kit's token.

!!! warning
	Check this guide to change the [Wi-Fi credentials](https://docs.smartcitizen.me/guides/getting-started/updating-wifi-credentials.md)!

## Connect to the Kit

- Click your **kit button** until the kit is in [setup mode](https://docs.smartcitizen.me/hardware/kit/features.md#setup-mode), the LED should be red.

![](https://docs.smartcitizen.me/assets/images/sck-setup.jpg)

- **Connect to the kit** with your mobile device as you did during the installation process. You will need to search for a **Wi-Fi network** called `SmartCitizen[···]`. If you have multiple kits `[···]` is the unique identifier of your kit.

- Once connected **you should see the Setup App in your phone**. If it doesn't show up automatically you can open http://192.168.1.1 on your phone browser.

!!! info
	The process also works on laptops, desktops and tablets, connected than can connect to the `SmartCitizen[···]` network.

## Update the token

- On the app **choose start** to move to the next page.

![](https://docs.smartcitizen.me/assets/images/onboarding-phone.png)

- You will see your **device key** or **token**. You can change your token in this page

![](https://docs.smartcitizen.me/assets/images/onboarding-token.png)

- On the next screen, you will see your Wi-Fi credentials, you can change them or leave them as they are!

![](https://docs.smartcitizen.me/assets/images/onboarding-wifi.png)

- The LED will go blue and **your kit should start to publish again using the new Wi-Fi**. You can confirm it by visiting your device on [smartcitizen.me/kits](https://smartcitizen.me/kits). You can repeat the process as many times as you want by going back to step 1.

!!! info
	If your Kit is not publishing check the [LED status chart](https://docs.smartcitizen.me/hardware/kit/features.md#operation-modes) to know more about it.
