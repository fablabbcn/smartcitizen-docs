# Resources for educators

Here there is a compilation of projects that we have done around education, directly or indirectly with other partners.

Typically, you can use the Smart Citizen Kit at quite different ages. Some more advanced materials require concepts to be previously acquired in other subjects or years. Each section below contains resources for different themes, ranging from general natural science and environmental topics, to air pollution or oceanography and data.

![](/assets/images/doit.png)
_Image credit: [DOIT](https://doit-europe.net)_

## Measuring our environment

!!! info "About this resource"
    **Resource:** [DOIT Toolkit - Measuring our environment (EN)- Future Learning Unit at Fablab Barcelona](/assets/toolkits/10_Measuring Our Environment_DOIT-Manual_en-2.pdf)

    **Duration**: 28h - 8 units
    **Setting**: Inside School
    **Group size**: 20-25 students
    **Age**: 14-15 years old

This resource presents activities that can be used to apply _maker_ education practices and open design for students to collect data and use it as a transformative tool for their immediate environment.

![](/assets/images/measuring-our-environment.png)

!!! info "School project in Barcelona?"
    You can have a look [here](https://serveiseducatius.xtec.cat/cesire/prestec/sensors-smart-citizen/) to borrow sensors and SCKs for a period of time with activities and other guides included.

## Aulamar

!!! info "About this resource"
    **Resource:** [Aulamar - the classroom to the sea - school project for coastal oceanography](/assets/toolkits/Aulamar_Secuencia_Didactica.pdf) (only in Spanish)

    * **Duration**: 8h-14h - 4-5 units
    * **Setting**: Inside School and in the beach
    * **Group size**: 20-25 students
    * **Age**: Adaptable to various ages, generally secondary school.

    This material was created as part of AULAMAR (Fundació Bit Habitat, ref. ID253) in collaboration with the Institut de Ciències del Mar (ICM - CSIC, Barcelona).

This resource contains a didactic sequence with activities to bring the class to the coast, and learn about oceanography and sensors.
